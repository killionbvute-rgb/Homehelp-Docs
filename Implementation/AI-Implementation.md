# Homehelp AI Implementation

## 1. Introduction

The Homehelp AI capability provides intelligence services that support learner understanding.

At the current stage, AI capabilities have been architecturally designed but are not yet implemented as production code.

The current implementation focus has been establishing:

* domain foundations
* learner understanding models
* conversation intelligence concepts
* responsible AI principles

The guiding principle:

> AI enhances understanding. It does not replace human judgement.

# 5. Conversation Intelligence

The first AI capability planned is Conversation Intelligence.

Purpose:

Transform natural conversations into structured learner understanding.

Example:

Input:

```text id="s2m7q4"
"My child enjoys building things but struggles with reading confidence."
```

AI interpretation:

```text id="w9x3k6"
Interest:
- Building activities

Challenge:
- Reading confidence

Potential observation:
- Explore confidence-building learning approaches
```

The domain validates meaning.
