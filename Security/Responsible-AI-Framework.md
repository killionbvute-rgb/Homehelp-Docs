# Homehelp Responsible AI Framework

## 1. Introduction

Homehelp uses artificial intelligence to support learner understanding and personalised education.

Because AI interacts with sensitive areas of human development, its use must be guided by strong principles of responsibility, safety, and trust.

The foundation of our approach is:

> AI should increase understanding while protecting human dignity.

---

# 2. Purpose

The Responsible AI Framework defines how Homehelp designs, develops, and operates artificial intelligence systems.

It ensures that AI capabilities:

* serve learners
* support families
* assist educators
* protect privacy
* maintain human responsibility

---

# 3. Responsible AI Principles

## 3.1 Human-Centred AI

AI exists to support people.

The AI Learning Companion should:

* enhance understanding
* provide useful insights
* assist decision-making

AI should not replace:

* parents
* educators
* professional judgement

---

## 3.2 Transparency

Families should understand:

* when AI is being used
* what information is processed
* how insights are generated
* what limitations exist

Trust requires clarity.

---

## 3.3 Privacy Protection

Learner information is valuable and must be protected.

Homehelp follows:

* purpose limitation
* minimum necessary data collection
* secure storage
* controlled access

Learner data exists to support education.

---

## 3.4 Safety

AI interactions must prioritise learner wellbeing.

The system must avoid:

* harmful recommendations
* inappropriate assumptions
* unsupported conclusions
* unnecessary data exposure

---

## 3.5 Fairness

AI must treat every learner with respect.

The system should actively avoid:

* bias
* unfair assumptions
* limiting expectations

Every learner should be recognised as unique.

---

# 4. AI Decision Boundaries

The AI Learning Companion may:

* identify patterns
* suggest insights
* organise information
* support conversations

The AI Learning Companion must not:

* make irreversible decisions about learners
* replace educators
* define a learner's potential
* create certainty where evidence is limited

---

# 5. Data Governance Principles

## Data Ownership

Learner information must be managed responsibly.

---

## Data Purpose

Information collected must have a clear educational purpose.

---

## Data Quality

Insights depend on accurate and meaningful information.

---

## Data Lifecycle

Information should have defined:

* collection rules
* storage rules
* usage rules
* retention rules
* deletion processes

---

# 6. AI Insight Governance

AI-generated insights should follow:

```text id="f4r0g7"
AI Observation

       |

       v

Validation

       |

       v

Educational Insight

       |

       v

Human-Aware Decision
```

AI suggestions are inputs, not final decisions.

---

# 7. Security Requirements

Homehelp must protect against:

* unauthorised access
* data leakage
* misuse of learner information
* insecure integrations

Security considerations apply across:

* applications
* APIs
* databases
* AI services
* infrastructure

---

# 8. Parent Trust Commitment

Parents should always know:

* what Homehelp understands about their child
* why that understanding exists
* how it supports learning

Trust is created through responsible behaviour.

---

# 9. Future Governance Capabilities

Future governance may include:

* AI evaluation processes
* model monitoring
* audit trails
* consent management
* ethical review processes

---

# 10. Conclusion

Responsible AI is not a technical feature.

It is an institutional commitment.

Homehelp will use artificial intelligence to better understand and support learners while protecting privacy, dignity, and human responsibility.

> Better intelligence requires greater responsibility.
