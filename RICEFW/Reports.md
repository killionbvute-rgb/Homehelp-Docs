# Homehelp Reports Catalogue

## 1. Introduction

Reports provide structured information that helps parents, educators, and institutional leaders understand learner journeys.

Homehelp reporting is designed around insight rather than simple data presentation.

The guiding principle:

> Reports should create understanding, not just display information.
