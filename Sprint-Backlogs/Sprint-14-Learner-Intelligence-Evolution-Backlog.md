# Sprint 14 Backlog

## Learner Intelligence Evolution

---

# 1. Sprint Overview

Sprint 14 continues the intelligence evolution of the AI Learning Companion.

Previous sprints progressively established:

- Learner understanding
- Knowledge evolution
- Conversation Intelligence
- Memory and Context Intelligence
- Learning Intelligence Synthesis
- Adaptive Conversation Intelligence

Sprint 14 extends these capabilities by introducing Learner Intelligence Evolution.

The sprint focuses on representing how learner understanding evolves over time through accumulated evidence and intelligence signals.

---

# 2. Sprint Objective

Implement the capability foundation that enables the AI Learning Companion to represent and evolve its understanding of a learner while preserving explainability, bounded intelligence and responsible AI behaviour.

---

# 3. Capability Scope

Sprint 14 includes work related to:

- learner intelligence evolution
- learner understanding progression
- intelligence evidence relationships
- confidence evolution
- learner development patterns

The sprint extends existing intelligence capabilities rather than replacing them.

# 4. Business Outcomes

Sprint 14 should improve the AI Learning Companion's ability to develop a richer understanding of learners over time.

Expected outcomes include:

## Outcome 1 — Progressive Learner Understanding

The system should improve its understanding of the learner through accumulated interactions rather than isolated observations.

---

## Outcome 2 — Evidence-Based Intelligence

Learner understanding should increasingly reflect accumulated evidence rather than individual conversations.

---

## Outcome 3 — Explainable Intelligence Growth

Parents and future platform capabilities should be able to understand how learner understanding has evolved.

---

## Outcome 4 — Better Personalisation Foundation

Future personalised guidance should benefit from a richer and more consistent learner understanding model.

---

## Outcome 5 — Responsible Intelligence Evolution

Learner understanding should evolve through governed intelligence processes without autonomous educational decision making.

---

# 9. Out of Scope

Sprint 14 focuses specifically on establishing Learner Intelligence Evolution capability boundaries.

The following areas are intentionally excluded from this sprint.

---

# 9.1 Autonomous Educational Decisions

Sprint 14 will not introduce:

- autonomous learning decisions
- autonomous intervention selection
- automatic changes to learner pathways
- independent educational recommendations without human oversight

Reason:

The AI Learning Companion must remain explainable and human-centred.

---

# 9.2 Curriculum Intelligence

Sprint 14 will not introduce:

- curriculum generation
- syllabus alignment
- automated lesson planning
- subject mastery determination

Reason:

Curriculum intelligence represents a separate future capability.

---

# 9.3 School Integration

Sprint 14 will not introduce:

- teacher workflows
- school reporting
- institutional accounts
- curriculum administration
- school data exchange

Reason:

School Integration requires separate discovery and bounded context definition.

---

# 9.4 Multi-Learner Family Support

Sprint 14 will not introduce:

- family relationship modelling
- multiple learner management
- household intelligence views
- family-level insights

Reason:

Family intelligence requires dedicated identity, privacy and relationship modelling.

---

# 9.5 Personalised Learning Plans

Sprint 14 will not introduce:

- formal learning plans
- automated learning schedules
- goal management workflows
- progress commitments

Reason:

Personalised learning plans should emerge from mature learner intelligence capabilities.

---

# 9.6 AI Model Optimisation

Sprint 14 will not focus on:

- model selection
- provider optimisation
- prompt tuning strategies
- infrastructure scaling

Reason:

The capability must remain independent from AI implementation choices.

---

# 9.7 Scope Protection Principle

Sprint 14 must remain focused on:

> Understanding how learner intelligence evolves, not deciding what the learner should do.

Any future capability requiring:

- external stakeholders
- autonomous decisions
- institutional workflows
- major domain expansion

must undergo a separate evolution review.
