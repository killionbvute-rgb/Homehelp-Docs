# Homehelp Operational Model

## 1. Introduction

Homehelp operates as a technology-enabled learning institution.

The platform is not only responsible for delivering software capabilities.

It is responsible for creating a trusted environment where learners, parents, educators, and technology work together.

The operating principle:

> Technology enables the institution. People create the impact.

---

# 2. Operational Philosophy

Homehelp operations are guided by:

* learner-centred thinking
* responsible AI usage
* continuous improvement
* transparency
* trust

Every operational decision should support better learner outcomes.

---

Homehelp operations consist of:

# 4. Learner Support Operations

## Purpose

Ensure learners receive meaningful and responsible support.

Activities include:

* reviewing learner insights
* monitoring learning journeys
* supporting personalised approaches
* identifying opportunities for improvement

---

# 5. Parent Engagement Operations

Parents are active partners.

Operations include:

* discovery conversations
* insight sharing
* feedback collection
* relationship management

The goal is ongoing partnership.
