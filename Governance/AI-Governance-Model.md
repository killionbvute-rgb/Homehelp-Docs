# Homehelp AI Governance Model

## 1. Introduction

Artificial intelligence is a core capability of Homehelp.

Because AI influences how learners are understood and supported, its development and operation require clear governance.

AI governance ensures that innovation remains aligned with:

* learner wellbeing
* parent trust
* educational responsibility
* institutional values

The guiding principle:

> Powerful technology requires responsible stewardship.

---

# 2. Purpose

The AI Governance Model defines how Homehelp manages:

* AI decisions
* AI development
* AI evaluation
* AI risks
* AI improvements

---

# 3. Governance Principles

## 3.1 Human Responsibility

AI assists people.

Humans remain responsible for:

* decisions
* interpretation
* learner support
* institutional outcomes

---

## 3.2 Transparency

Homehelp should maintain visibility into:

* where AI is used
* what AI produces
* limitations of AI outputs

---

## 3.3 Safety

AI capabilities must be evaluated for:

* harmful outputs
* incorrect interpretations
* inappropriate recommendations

---

## 3.4 Accountability

Every AI capability should have:

* ownership
* monitoring
* review processes

---

# 7. AI Evaluation Framework

AI systems should be evaluated across:

## Accuracy

Does the system understand correctly?

---

## Safety

Does it avoid harmful outcomes?

---

## Fairness

Does it treat learners appropriately?

---

## Usefulness

Does it improve educational support?

---

## Trust

Do families understand and accept its role?

---

# 8. Model Change Management

AI changes should be controlled.

Changes may require:

* evaluation
* testing
* documentation
* approval

Significant AI changes should not happen without understanding impact.

# 10. Risk Management

AI risks include:

* inaccurate insights
* bias
* privacy concerns
* over-reliance on automation

Governance ensures risks are identified and managed.

---

# 11. Future Governance Capabilities

Future capabilities may include:

* AI audit processes
* model performance dashboards
* ethical review committees
* AI incident response procedures

---

# 12. Conclusion

AI governance ensures that Homehelp grows responsibly.

The purpose is not to slow innovation.

The purpose is to make innovation worthy of trust.

> Responsible AI is a foundation of institutional excellence.
