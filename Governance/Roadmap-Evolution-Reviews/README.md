# Roadmap Evolution Reviews

## Purpose

Roadmap Evolution Reviews capture strategic learning that emerges during execution and may influence future roadmap direction.

They provide a governed mechanism for recording:

- capability evolution
- execution discoveries
- architectural learning
- delivery sequencing changes
- emerging strategic opportunities
- roadmap alignment observations

---

# Relationship With Other Governance Artifacts

Roadmap Evolution Reviews complement, but do not replace, existing governance documents.

## Roadmap Evolution Reviews

Location:

`Governance/Roadmap-Evolution-Reviews/`

Purpose:

Capture strategic learning discovered during execution.

They answer:

- Did implementation reveal new capability boundaries?
- Did architectural understanding evolve?
- Does future roadmap sequencing require review?
- Did delivery reality differ from original assumptions?

---

# Evolution Review Principles

## 1. Roadmap Stability

The execution roadmap remains the authoritative strategic plan.

Execution discoveries should first be recorded as learning.

A roadmap revision occurs only when:

- strategic direction changes
- capability boundaries change
- delivery sequencing materially changes

---

## 2. Evidence-Based Evolution

Roadmap evolution decisions must be supported by evidence.

Evidence may include:

- Sprint Completion Reviews
- Architecture Decision Records
- Domain Design Reviews
- Automated validation results
- Product learning

---

## 3. Architecture Alignment

Roadmap evolution must preserve alignment between:

Capability
|
v
Bounded Context
|
v
Domain Model
|
v
Application Workflow
|
v
Implementation Evidence

---

## 4. Traceability

Every roadmap evolution review should reference:

- originating capability
- related sprint
- affected ADRs
- affected DDRs
- implementation evidence
- resulting decision

---

# Review Lifecycle

Roadmap Evolution Reviews follow:

Execution Learning
    |
Capability Observation
    |
Architecture Review
    |
Governance Decision
    |
Roadmap Confirmation or Revision
