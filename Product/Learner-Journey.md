# Homehelp Learner Journey

## 1. Introduction

The Homehelp learner journey describes how learners and families experience the institution from their first interaction through continuous learning support.

The journey is built around one central principle:

> Every learner deserves to be understood before they are supported.

The learner journey transforms information into understanding, and understanding into personalised educational support.

---

# 2. Journey Overview

The Homehelp experience follows these stages:

```text
Introduction

     |

Discovery

     |

Learner Understanding

     |

Living Learner Profile

     |

Personalised Support

     |

Continuous Growth
```

---

# 3. Stage One: Introduction

## Purpose

Create trust and establish the relationship with the family.

The first interaction should communicate:

* who Homehelp is
* why understanding matters
* how learner information is used responsibly

---

## Family Experience

Parents should feel:

* listened to
* respected
* confident
* understood

The goal is relationship building, not information collection.

---

# 4. Stage Two: Discovery

## Purpose

Understand the learner before making recommendations.

Discovery explores:

* learner interests
* strengths
* challenges
* learning preferences
* goals
* parent observations

---

## Discovery Approach

The process uses meaningful conversations rather than only forms or questionnaires.

The AI Companion helps guide conversations while maintaining a human-centred approach.

---

# 5. Stage Three: Learner Understanding

## Purpose

Transform discovered information into meaningful learner knowledge.

Information is analysed and organised into:

* learner characteristics
* strengths
* challenges
* preferences
* goals

---

## Sources of Understanding

Learner understanding may come from:

* parent conversations
* learner conversations
* educator observations
* assessments
* ongoing interactions

---

# 6. Stage Four: Living Learner Profile

## Purpose

Create a continuously evolving representation of the learner.

The Learner Profile is not a static record.

It grows as Homehelp learns more about the learner.

---

## Profile Areas

```text
Identity

Strengths

Challenges

Learning Preferences

Goals

Progress Insights

Observations
```

---

# 7. Stage Five: Personalised Support

## Purpose

Use learner understanding to provide meaningful support.

Support may include:

* learning recommendations
* educational guidance
* parent insights
* progress conversations

---

## Guiding Principle

Support should be based on:

"Who this learner is"

rather than:

"What usually works for learners like this"

---

# 8. Stage Six: Continuous Growth

## Purpose

Maintain an ongoing relationship with the learner.

Learning is not a one-time event.

The AI Companion continues learning through:

* conversations
* progress updates
* new observations
* changing goals

---

# 9. Parent Experience

Parents should receive:

## Understanding

Clear insights into their child's learning profile.

---

## Guidance

Support in helping their child grow.

---

## Confidence

Trust that technology is being used responsibly.

---

# 10. Learner Experience

Learners should experience:

* recognition of their uniqueness
* support aligned with their needs
* encouragement of their strengths
* confidence in their abilities

---

# 11. Institutional Value

The learner journey creates value by connecting:

```text
Family Knowledge

        +

AI Understanding

        +

Educational Expertise

        =

Personalised Learning Support
```

---

# 12. Future Evolution

Future journey stages may include:

* adaptive learning plans
* educator collaboration
* learner self-reflection
* achievement tracking
* personalised learning pathways

---

# 13. Conclusion

The Homehelp learner journey represents a shift from education that treats learners as groups to education that understands learners as individuals.

The journey begins with discovery.

Discovery creates understanding.

Understanding creates better support.

> Know the learner. Support the learner. Empower the learner.
