# 2. Sprint Context

The AI Learning Companion has progressively developed intelligence capabilities across previous sprints:

- Learner Understanding Foundation
- Learner Knowledge Evolution
- Learner Intelligence and Guidance
- Parent Trust and Explainability
- AI Interaction Intelligence
- AI Memory and Context Intelligence
- AI Learning Intelligence Synthesis
- Adaptive Conversation Intelligence

Sprint 13 established the ability to evaluate conversation effectiveness and recommend future communication improvements while maintaining strict boundaries against autonomous behaviour modification.

The next evolutionary question is how accumulated intelligence signals become a continuously evolving understanding of the learner.

---

# 3. Architectural Question

Sprint 14 addresses the question:

> How does the AI Learning Companion transform accumulated learner knowledge, interaction history and intelligence signals into an evolving model of learner understanding?

---

# 4. Sprint Objective

Sprint 14 introduces Learner Intelligence Evolution.

The objective is to establish the capability foundation for representing how learner understanding changes over time through:

- accumulated evidence
- intelligence signals
- observed patterns
- confidence evolution

The capability should strengthen personalised guidance while preserving responsible AI boundaries.

---

# 5. Capability Boundary

## Capability Responsibility

Learner Intelligence Evolution is responsible for:

- representing evolution of learner understanding
- tracking intelligence progression
- connecting evidence with learner understanding
- maintaining explainable evolution history
- identifying emerging learner patterns

---

## Capability Does Not Own

Learner Intelligence Evolution does not own:

- learner identity
- learner profile management
- conversation processing
- memory storage
- prompt orchestration
- curriculum decisions
- autonomous learning decisions
- school workflows
- family relationship management

---

---

# 7. Domain Intent

Sprint 14 will explore concepts relating to:

## Learner Understanding Evolution

How learner understanding changes over time.

---

## Intelligence Evidence

How observations and signals contribute to confidence in understanding.

---

## Intelligence Confidence

How certainty and reliability of understanding are represented.

---

## Learning Patterns

How recurring learner behaviours and characteristics emerge.

---

# 8. Success Measure

Sprint 14 is successful when:

> The AI Learning Companion can maintain an explainable model of how learner understanding evolves over time, using accumulated intelligence signals and evidence, without autonomous educational decision making.

---

# 9. Non-Goals

Sprint 14 will not introduce:

- automated curriculum generation
- autonomous learner interventions
- school integration
- teacher collaboration workflows
- multi-learner family management
- external institutional integrations
- production-scale optimisation

These remain future capability areas.

---

# 10. Governance Requirements

Sprint 14 must maintain:

- explainable intelligence evolution
- human oversight
- bounded AI behaviour
- capability independence
- domain ownership clarity
- traceability between evidence and intelligence
