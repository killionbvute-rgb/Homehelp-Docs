# Sprint Goal

Transform Knowledge Evolution from a historical recording mechanism into an intelligent capability that continuously improves Homehelp's understanding of each learner over time.

The sprint will strengthen the learner's evolving knowledge model while preserving explainability, auditability and responsible AI principles.

---

# Business Outcome

At the end of Sprint 06, Homehelp should not simply remember learner observations.

It should begin understanding how learner knowledge evolves over time, allowing future AI guidance to become increasingly personalised, evidence-based and trustworthy.

---

# Capability Objectives

## Objective 1 — Strengthen the Knowledge Evolution Domain

Improve the domain model so that learner knowledge evolution represents meaningful change rather than isolated events.

Expected outcomes include:

* richer evolution records
* improved domain consistency
* complete lifecycle representation of learner knowledge

---

## Objective 2 — Capture Learner Change

Enable the platform to represent changes in learner understanding rather than static observations.

Examples include:

* emerging strengths
* emerging challenges
* changing learning preferences
* evolving learning goals
* recurring behavioural patterns

---

## Objective 3 — Establish Evolution Intelligence Foundations

Prepare the architecture for future intelligence capabilities including:

* trend detection
* confidence progression
* repeated observation recognition
* contradiction detection
* long-term learner understanding

These foundations may not all be implemented during this sprint but the architecture should support them.

---

## Objective 4 — Preserve Explainability

Every evolution of learner knowledge must remain explainable.

Parents, educators and future AI components should always be able to understand:

* what changed
* why it changed
* which evidence supported the change

# Acceptance Criteria

The sprint will be considered complete when:

* Knowledge Evolution accurately represents learner change over time.
* Domain inconsistencies identified during capability review have been resolved.
* Knowledge Evolution integrates cleanly with Learner Insights.
* Evolution history supports future intelligence capabilities.
* Traceability remains fully aligned.
* All automated tests pass.
* Documentation remains current.

---

# Definition of Done

The capability is complete when:

* Domain implementation is complete.
* Application workflows are aligned.
* Repository contracts remain consistent.
* Automated tests succeed.
* Documentation is updated where required.
* Architecture remains aligned with Product Execution Roadmap v2.

# Risks

Potential implementation risks include:

* introducing duplicate learner knowledge
* reducing explainability
* weakening aggregate boundaries
* creating tightly coupled intelligence services

Implementation decisions should favour clear bounded contexts, strong domain modelling and long-term maintainability.
